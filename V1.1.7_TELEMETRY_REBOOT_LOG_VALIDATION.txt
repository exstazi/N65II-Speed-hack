N65II v1.1.7 — BROAD 4/6/7 TELEMETRY + REBOOT LOGGER — VALIDATION
===================================================================

Purpose
-------
Fix v1.1.6's incorrect early-stop assumption that 0x70 DATA[26] must echo the requested 0x58 mode.
The supplied hardware log showed baseline, mode 4, mode 6, mode 7 and even restore mode 5 all reading back as driveMode=0.
Therefore v1.1.7 treats DATA[26] as non-decisive for this 6001 / Meter 2058 diagnostic.

Diagnostic sequence
-------------------
1. Capture baseline while stationary.
2. Send 0x58=4, capture raw B003 + parsed NAVEE frames, then restore 0x58=5.
3. Send 0x58=6, capture, then restore 0x58=5.
4. Send 0x58=7, capture, then restore 0x58=5.
5. Compare latest 0x70/0x90/0x91/0x92 frames with baseline.
6. Select the candidate with the strongest observable telemetry delta; ties prefer 4.
7. If no candidate produced any observable delta, fallback to mode 4 because BLDC0005 has the known dedicated mode-4 branch.
8. Re-apply the selected candidate, capture a final pre-reboot snapshot.
9. ALWAYS enter WAIT_POWER_CYCLE and show STARTA OM NU.
10. After a physical OFF/ON + reconnect, CHECK AFTER REBOOT captures broad telemetry again.
11. Export includes event log, raw 0x70 snapshots, parsed frame snapshots, and raw B003 notification chunks.

Important behavior
------------------
- The logger never enters DFU and never flashes firmware.
- The logger no longer marks mode4Verified or mode4VolatileVerified from DATA[26] alone.
- Firmware release preflight remains blocked after the diagnostic until the evidence is reviewed.
- Movement >0.5 km/h aborts the sweep.
- Unexpected disconnect during active capture stops the sweep but preserves captured evidence.
- Disconnect while WAIT_POWER_CYCLE is expected and preserved as part of the reboot procedure.
